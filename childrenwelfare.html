Empowering Children Through Care & Education

The children of today will shape the future of the country. Their upbringing determines the country's future. Pandit Jawaharlal Nehru emphasized that the way we nurture them shapes the future. Education is crucial, and in today's world, it's a key for growth and success.

Tapasvi, with the ideology of providing modern education to economically deprived and challenged children, aims to create well-rounded individuals through education, life skills, and value development.

Empowering Children Through Education & Care

The children of today shape the future of tomorrow. Their upbringing, education, and values determine the progress of society.

Tapasvi believes every child deserves access to education, opportunities, guidance, and holistic development regardless of economic background.

Akshara Jyothi Project

Tapasvi’s Akshara Jyothi Project supports children living in underserved communities by providing educational materials, tutoring, and academic support.

Value Education

Personality development sessions, creative activities, games, arts, crafts, and spiritual teachings help children build strong values and confidence.

Trainings & Workshops

Workshops focus on communication, leadership, emotional intelligence, self-defense, and essential life skills.

Sports Academy for Children

Sports teach discipline, teamwork, leadership, resilience, and confidence. Tapasvi’s Sports Academy empowers youth, especially children from economically disadvantaged backgrounds.

Since December 2022, Tapasvi has organized inter-school tournaments for children under 14 in Kho-Kho, Kabaddi, and Volleyball across Armoor and Nizamabad.

Through TAPASVI CUPS and structured coaching, children are encouraged to grow physically, mentally, and socially while learning teamwork and leadership.

Sports Workshops & Training

Tapasvi conducts specialized workshops focusing on tactical strategies, leadership in sports, team coordination, and mental strength required for competitive sports.
