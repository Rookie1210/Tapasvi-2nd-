Goshala

Caring For Cows With Compassion, Protection, and Sustainable Support.

Cow Protection & Goshaala

Sheltering old, abandoned, and unproductive cows has been an ancient practice in India. Tapasvi’s Goshaala is dedicated to protecting and caring for cows with compassion, dignity, and sustainable support.

Protecting cows contributes to biodiversity, natural farming, and traditional eco-friendly practices. Panchagavya products such as milk, curd, dung, urine, and ghee are used in farming, healthcare, and spiritual practices.

Preserving a Traditional Practice

Since independence, India has attempted to establish shelters and Gosadans for protecting uneconomic cattle. However, lack of resources, land, transportation, and infrastructure posed major challenges.

Tapasvi continues this compassionate tradition by creating a peaceful, clean, and caring environment for cows while encouraging awareness about sustainable cattle care.

Cleaning & Maintenance

Bathing cows, cleaning shelters, healthcare inspections, vaccination, and regular medical attention are essential parts of our daily seva.

Our Herd

Our Goshaala currently houses indigenous Desi cows known for producing healthy A2 milk and contributing to sustainable living.

Feeding & Care

Fresh fodder, mineral supplements, and regular grazing ensure the cows receive proper nutrition, care, and a healthy environment.

Community Participation

Visitors are encouraged to learn about Indian cow breeds, their ecological importance, and their contribution to society.

Those interested can participate in grooming cows, feeding them, and supporting the maintenance of the Goshaala.

Go Daana (Donate a Cow)

Go Daana is considered one of the highest forms of donation in Indian tradition. Contributions towards cow protection, fodder, healthcare, and shelter are welcomed with gratitude.
