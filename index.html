Who We Are

We are dedicated to creating meaningful change through elder care, children welfare, women empowerment, environmental conservation and community service.

Our Mission

To serve communities through compassion, care, empowerment, and sustainable initiatives that create meaningful social impact.

Our Vision

To build an inclusive and empowered society where every individual has the opportunity to live with dignity and hope.

Our Goals

To support elders, nurture children, empower women, conserve resources, and strengthen communities through service.

Our Programs

Elder's Care Centre

Children's Welfare Centre

Supporting children through education, care, and development initiatives.

Women Empowerment

Encouraging confidence, independence, and equal opportunities for women.

Goshala
