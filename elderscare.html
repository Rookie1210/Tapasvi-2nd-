Elder's Care Centre

Supporting Senior Citizens With Care

Tapasvi NGO’s Elder’s Care Centre is dedicated to supporting elderly individuals through compassionate care, emotional support, and community engagement.

We strive to create a safe and respectful environment where senior citizens feel valued, supported, and connected.

Challenges Faced by Senior Citizens

Old age is an integral part of human life, yet it often becomes one of the most difficult and vulnerable stages.

  • Physical Problems: Deterioration of health and chronic illness
  • Cognitive Problems: Dementia and depression
  • Emotional Problems: Loneliness, isolation, and loss of companionship
  • Social Problems: Limited social interaction and fear of isolation
  • Financial Problems: Lack of financial security and support

At Tapasvi NGO, we believe every senior citizen deserves care, dignity, emotional support, healthcare, and a peaceful environment to age gracefully.

Compassionate Care & Support

Tapasvi serves poor, abandoned, and helpless senior citizens who require assistance with daily living and emotional support.

Our compassionate caregivers and staff ensure special attention is given to their diet, shelter, safety, healthcare, and medication within a peaceful and harmonious environment.

We also extend additional support whenever required by arranging spiritual sessions, dedicated caregivers, medical assistance, physiotherapy, and nursing services to ensure every senior citizen receives dignity, comfort, and compassionate care.
